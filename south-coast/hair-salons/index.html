Common questions

Are hair stylists and barbers licensed in Oregon?

Yes. Hair design and barbering are licensed professions in Oregon, overseen by the Oregon Health Licensing Office. You can look up any practitioner or salon license on the state's public license search.

How is this list put together?

Compiled from public business data (Overture Maps, Nov 2026 release), with national chains like Great Clips and Supercuts filtered out and closed or duplicate listings removed.
