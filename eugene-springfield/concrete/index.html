Before you hire

Concrete and paving work over $1,000 in Oregon requires an active CCB license. Longevity comes from what you cannot see once it is poured: base prep, thickness, reinforcement and control joints. Ask each bidder to spell those out, along with cure time before you drive on it and who handles any old concrete removal.

Concrete & Paving in Eugene & Springfield: common questions

Do concrete contractors in Oregon need a license?
Yes, for work over $1,000 - an active CCB license is required. Every listing on this page is checked against CCB records.
When can concrete be poured in the Willamette Valley?
Most of the year, but heavy rain and freezing nights complicate finishing and curing. Spring through fall is the main season for driveways and flatwork.
What makes one concrete bid cheaper than another?
Usually base prep, thickness, reinforcement and finish. A bid that skips base prep is cheaper for a reason - ask each bidder to list those four items specifically.
