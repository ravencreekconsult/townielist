Oakridge · town page

Pinard Falls

Hiking trail · Oakridge · official info
Short forest trail to a 105-foot waterfall pouring through a notch in the basalt

Salt Creek Falls

Waterfall · Oakridge · Free entry · official info
Oregon's second-highest single-drop waterfall at 286 ft, with a wheelchair-accessible observation platform near the parking area
Day use sunrise to sunset; observation site closed in winter (park at Salt Creek Sno-Park)
Northwest Forest Pass or interagency pass required
