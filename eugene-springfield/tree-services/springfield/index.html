Before you hire

Tree work is one of the highest-risk trades for homeowners: an uninsured climber hurt on your property can become your liability. Oregon requires a CCB license for tree work over $1,000 - verify it free at ccb.state.or.us and ask for a certificate of insurance naming you. For large removals near structures, ask whether they carry a certified arborist on staff and get the cleanup and stump handling in writing.

Tree Services in Springfield: common questions

How much does tree removal cost in Eugene?
Small trees often run $300-$800; large removals near structures commonly run $1,500-$4,000+ in the Eugene/Springfield area. Storm-damaged or crane work costs more. Two written bids are worth it.
Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Eugene?
Sometimes. The City of Eugene regulates removal of certain street trees and significant trees. Check with the city or ask your tree service - established local companies know the rules.
When is the best time to trim trees in Oregon?
Late fall through winter, when trees are dormant, is best for most species. Dead or hazardous limbs should come off any time of year.
