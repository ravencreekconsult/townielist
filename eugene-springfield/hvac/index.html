Before you hire

Heating and cooling work in Oregon requires an active CCB license, and gas furnace work should always be permitted and inspected. Ask whether the bid includes the permit, the old unit haul-away, and the start-up checklist. For heat pumps, ask about current Energy Trust of Oregon incentives - they change and can take real money off a qualifying install.
