Before you hire

In Oregon, pest control companies are licensed by the Oregon Department of Agriculture, not the CCB - ask for their ODA license and verify the applicators. For ants, spiders and rodents, ask whether the quote is one-time or a recurring plan, what products are used, and what the guarantee covers if pests come back between visits.

Pest Control in Eugene: common questions

Who licenses pest control companies in Oregon?
The Oregon Department of Agriculture licenses pest control operators and applicators. Ask for the license number and verify it before work starts.
Is a pest control plan worth it?
For recurring issues like ants and spiders, a quarterly plan often costs less than repeated one-time visits. Ask what each visit covers and what the guarantee includes.
What pests are common in the Eugene area?
Carpenter ants, odorous house ants, spiders, rodents, and moisture-loving pests are the common calls in the Willamette Valley. A good local company will identify the pest before quoting treatment.
