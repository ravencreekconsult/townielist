Common questions

Do auto repair shops in Oregon need a license?

Oregon does not issue a state license for general auto repair the way it does for construction trades. What to look for instead: ASE-certified technicians, a written estimate before work starts, and clear warranty terms. Every shop on this page is an independent local business - national chains are excluded.

How much does a mechanic charge in Eugene?

Independent shops in the Eugene-Springfield area typically charge about $95 to $150 per labor hour, generally less than dealership service departments. Ask for a written estimate that separates parts and labor before you authorize work.

How do I pick a good local mechanic?

Start with the written estimate: a good shop lists parts, labor and shop fees separately and calls before doing any work beyond it. Ask what warranty covers the repair - parts and labor, and for how long.
