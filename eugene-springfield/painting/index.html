Before you hire

In Oregon, painting work over $1,000 requires an active CCB license - verify it free at ccb.state.or.us. The difference between a cheap paint job and a lasting one is almost always prep: ask exactly what scraping, sanding, caulking and priming is in the bid, which paint line is quoted, and how many coats. Homes built before 1978 may involve lead-safe work practices.

Painting in Eugene & Springfield: common questions

Do painters in Oregon need a license?
Yes, for work over $1,000 - an active CCB license is required. Every listing on this page is checked against CCB records.
When is exterior painting season in Eugene?
The dry season, roughly May through October. Good local painters book out weeks ahead in summer, so schedule estimates in spring.
What should a painting bid include?
Prep steps, primer, paint brand and line, number of coats, areas included and excluded, cleanup, and warranty. Two or three written bids make the differences obvious.
