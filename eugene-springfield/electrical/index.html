Before you hire

In Oregon, an electrical contractor needs an active CCB license for work over $1,000, and the electrician doing the work holds a separate state journeyman license. Permits are required for most wiring work in Eugene and Springfield - ask whether the bid includes the permit and inspection. For panel upgrades and EV charger circuits, get the load calculation in writing.
