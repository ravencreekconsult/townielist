Before you hire

In Oregon, an electrical contractor needs an active CCB license for work over $1,000, and the electrician doing the work holds a separate state journeyman license. Permits are required for most wiring work in Eugene and Springfield - ask whether the bid includes the permit and inspection. For panel upgrades and EV charger circuits, get the load calculation in writing.

Electrical in Eugene: common questions

Do electricians in Oregon need a license?
Yes. Electrical contracting work over $1,000 requires an active CCB license, and the person doing the wiring needs a state electrical license. Every listing on this page is checked against CCB records.
Does electrical work need a permit in Eugene?
Most wiring work does - panel changes, new circuits, rewires and EV charger installs are all permitted work. Established local electricians handle the permit as part of the job.
How do I compare electrician bids?
Get two or three written bids that spell out the scope, the panel or parts brand, permit fees and the labor warranty. The cheapest line item quote often leaves the permit out.
