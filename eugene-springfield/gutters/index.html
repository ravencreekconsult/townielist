Before you hire

Gutter installation over $1,000 in Oregon requires an active CCB license. In the Willamette Valley rain, sizing and slope matter more than brand: ask about gutter size, downspout count and placement, and whether the quote includes fascia repair if rot shows up. Get guards quoted separately so you can compare them honestly.

Gutters in Eugene & Springfield: common questions

Do gutter installers in Oregon need a license?
Yes, for work over $1,000 - an active CCB license is required. Every listing on this page is checked against CCB records.
Seamless or sectional gutters?
Most local installers form seamless gutters on site, which leak less than sectional. Ask what is being quoted and what the warranty covers.
Are gutter guards worth it under fir trees?
They help with needles and leaves, but no guard is maintenance-free. Ask each bidder which guard type they recommend for your trees and roof pitch, and why.
