Before you hire

Oregon requires plumbers to hold a CCB license for work over $1,000 and a state plumbing license for the work itself. Always ask for both numbers and look the CCB number up free at ccb.state.or.us. For water heaters, repipes and sewer work, get at least two written bids and ask whether the quote includes permits - the City of Eugene and Springfield both require them for most water heater replacements.

Plumbing in Eugene & Springfield: common questions

How much does a plumber cost in Eugene?
Expect $85-$150 per hour for a licensed plumber in the Eugene/Springfield area, with flat-rate quotes common for water heaters and drain work. Emergency calls usually add a trip fee.
Do plumbers in Oregon need a license?
Yes. Construction work over $1,000 requires an active CCB license, and plumbing work itself requires a state plumbing license. Every listing on this page is checked against CCB records.
How fast can a plumber come out for an emergency?
Several Eugene/Springfield plumbers offer same-day or emergency service. Calling two or three from the list above is the fastest way to find an open slot today.
