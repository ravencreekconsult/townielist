Before you hire

Roofing is one of the most storm-damaged trades in the Willamette Valley, and one of the most bid-shopped. Oregon requires an active CCB license for roofing work - verify it free at ccb.state.or.us. Ask whether the bid is a tear-off or an overlay, what underlayment and ventilation are included, and get the workmanship warranty in writing, separate from the shingle manufacturer warranty.

Roofing in Eugene: common questions

Do roofers in Oregon need a license?
Yes. Roofing work requires an active CCB license. Every listing on this page is checked against CCB records.
How many roofing bids should I get?
Three written bids is the standard advice for a full replacement. Make sure each bid lists the same scope - tear-off, underlayment, flashing, ventilation and cleanup - so you can compare like for like.
When is roofing season in the Willamette Valley?
Dry months, roughly June through October, are the busy season and book out early. Many local roofers also do emergency repairs and dry-in work year-round.
