Common questions

Are national gym chains listed here?

No. Townie List only lists independent, locally owned gyms and studios - national chains are excluded so membership dollars stay local.

How is this list put together?

Compiled from public business data (Overture Maps, Nov 2026 release), with chains filtered out and closed or duplicate listings removed.
