Bend · town page

Pilot Butte

Scenic viewpoint · Bend · official info
In-town cinder cone with a summit road and trails to a panoramic view of the Cascades

Tumalo Falls

Waterfall · Bend · Free entry · official info
97-ft waterfall on Tumalo Creek, with a viewpoint a short walk from the trailhead
Recreation pass required: $5 day pass or Northwest Forest Pass

Cascade Lakes Scenic Byway

Scenic drive · Bend · official info
66-mile loop past a dozen Cascade lakes and trailheads west of Bend
Closed by snow in winter (usually Nov-late May); check Deschutes National Forest road conditions

Shevlin Park

Park · Bend · official info
Large ponderosa-pine park with trails along Tumalo Creek at the west edge of Bend

Drake Park

Park · Bend · official info
Downtown riverside park on Mirror Pond, with a broad lawn and looping path

Glass Butte

Rockhounding · Bend · official info
BLM rockhounding site on Hwy 20 east of Bend - free personal-use obsidian collecting

Hampton Butte

Rockhounding · Bend · official info
BLM wilderness study area east of Bend known for petrified wood; free personal-use rockhounding on public land

Deschutes River Trail

Hiking trail · Bend · official info
Riverside trail network through Bend along the Deschutes, linking parks, the Old Mill District and canyon stretches

La Pine · town page

Newberry Crater (Paulina & East Lakes)

Caldera lakes · La Pine · official info
Caldera lakes, the Big Obsidian Flow and lakeshore hot springs inside Newberry Volcano

Paulina Peak

Scenic viewpoint · La Pine · official info
7,984-ft summit with a seasonal road to the top of Newberry Volcano, overlooking the caldera

Fort Rock State Natural Area

Rock formation · La Pine · official info
Towering tuff-ring arena rising from an Ice Age lake bed southeast of La Pine
Day use only, 8am-4pm

Crater Lake National Park

National park · La Pine · Free entry · official info
Deepest lake in the US, inside the Mount Mazama caldera; Rim Drive typically open Jul-Oct
Entrance $30/vehicle mid-May through Oct ($15 Nov-mid-May), good for 7 days

Big Obsidian Flow

Volcanic monument · La Pine · Free entry · official info
One-mile interpretive trail across the youngest lava flow in Oregon (1,300 years), black obsidian glass and pumice in Newberry Caldera
Open June-late fall; ranger talks at the amphitheater in summer
Recreation pass required: $5/vehicle day pass or Northwest Forest Pass

Madras · town page

The Cove Palisades State Park

State park · Madras · Free entry · official info
Canyon-rim views, boating and swimming on Lake Billy Chinook southwest of Madras

Museum · Prineville · official info
Fossil museum and visitor center at the Sheep Rock Unit of John Day Fossil Beds National Monument
Outdoor trails and overlooks open at all times; see NPS for visitor center hours

Sunriver · town page

Lava Lands Visitor Center

Volcanic monument · Sunriver · Free entry · official info
Gateway to Newberry National Volcanic Monument: lava flows, interpretive trails and the Lava Butte summit shuttle
Open seasonally May 15-Sep (daily 9am-5pm to mid-Sep); summit shuttle mid-Jun-Labor Day
Parking $5/vehicle or Northwest Forest Pass

Lava River Cave

Cave · Sunriver · Free entry · official info
Self-guided mile-long lava tube inside Newberry National Volcanic Monument
Open early May-Sep 15; free timed reservation required ($2 processing fee on recreation.gov)
Parking $5/vehicle or Northwest Forest Pass
