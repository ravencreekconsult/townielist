Three Sisters Wilderness

Wilderness · Sisters · official info
Alpine lakes, lava fields and glaciated peaks; trailheads off the Cascade Lakes Hwy and McKenzie Pass
Central Cascades Wilderness day-use permit required Jun 15-Oct 15 ($1/person via recreation.gov)

Black Butte Trail

Hiking trail · Sisters · Free entry · official info
Steep 2-mile climb up a symmetrical volcano to panoramic Cascade views
Recreation pass required: $5/vehicle day pass or Northwest Forest Pass

Dee Wright Observatory

Observatory · Sisters · Free entry · official info
CCC-built lava-rock observatory atop McKenzie Pass with panoramic Cascade views
Seasonal - McKenzie Hwy 242 closed in winter
Free entry

Peterson Ridge Trail
