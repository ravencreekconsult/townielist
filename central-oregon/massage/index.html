Common questions

Are massage therapists licensed in Oregon?

Yes. Oregon licenses massage therapists (LMT) through the Oregon Board of Massage Therapists, which has a public license lookup. Licensing covers training hours, an exam and continuing education.

How is this list put together?

Compiled from public business data (Overture Maps, Nov 2026 release), with national chains like Massage Envy filtered out and closed or duplicate listings removed.
