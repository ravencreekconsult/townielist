Common questions

How do I check an Oregon dentist's license?

Dentists in Oregon are licensed by the Oregon Board of Dentistry, which runs a free public license lookup by name. Dental hygienists are licensed separately by the same board.

How is this list put together?

Compiled from public business data (Overture Maps, Nov 2026 release), with national and large regional chains filtered out and closed or duplicate listings removed.
